# Mutant Beans social publishing plan

This is a lightweight repeatable plan for discovery. It uses the band's existing music, lineup, live archive, Ensenada identity, and verified Ernie Ball / Warped Tour story. It does not publish to social accounts automatically.

## Weekly rhythm

- **One original vertical performance clip:** 9:16, usually 12–25 seconds. Put the strongest musical or visual moment in the first second, add readable captions, identify the song, and credit/tag the venue or event when appropriate.
- **One story or carousel:** rehearsal, a member detail, a crowd moment, gear, or a short note about a song. Use original images or footage and add descriptive alt text where the platform supports it.
- **For every show:** share the official date page, tag the venue/promoter, and post a short reminder before the show plus a live or post-show moment when footage is available.

## Four rotating themes

1. **The sound:** a live excerpt and a link to the featured song.
2. **The people:** one member or mascot moment, with the member's role and profile tagged.
3. **The place:** Ensenada/Baja California, rehearsal spaces, local scene, or a venue/crowd moment.
4. **The milestone:** the Ernie Ball Battle of the Bands win and the Long Beach Vans Warped Tour appearance, with the official campaign or press story linked when relevant.

## Caption structure

1. Start with a short hook tied to the actual clip.
2. Name the song, show, or member so the post has context outside the band's follower base.
3. Add one direct action: listen, share the show date, or follow the official page.
4. Use a small set of relevant tags and location tags; avoid filling the caption with unrelated keywords.

## Link tracking convention

Use the matching page and a consistent UTM label when sharing a link from a social profile or post. Example:

`https://mutantbeans.com/shows/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=show_dates`

Use `utm_source=youtube`, `tiktok`, or `facebook` for the corresponding platform. Keep the official untagged URL in the website, press kit, and venue profiles.

## Reuse

Export a clean master without platform watermarks, then upload it natively to each platform. Adapt the crop, caption, and CTA to that platform. Keep English and Spanish captions clear and natural; use a separate language version when a mixed caption becomes hard to scan.
