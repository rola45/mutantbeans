Ensenada, Baja California · since 2014

A decade of mutation

Mutant Beans grew out of Ensenada’s independent scene with a sound that crosses funk and rock with hip-hop, thrash, punk, and hardcore.

A sound of their own

From Ensenada. Against the current.

Formed in 2014, the band writes and performs with the energy of an independent group doing things its own way. Their music brings together funk-rock, hip-hop, thrash, punk, and hardcore, with songs that face social and political realities.
