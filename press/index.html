Band bio

A decade of mutation

Formed in Ensenada in 2014, Mutant Beans blend funk and rock with hip-hop, thrash, punk, and hardcore. Their songs face social and political realities with the energy of an independent band doing things their own way.

In 2026, Mutant Beans won Ernie Ball’s Battle of the Bands, earning a place at Vans Warped Tour in Long Beach, California.
